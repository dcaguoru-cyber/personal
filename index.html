About David

David is a UK-based Cyber Security and Digital Forensics graduate from Kingston University. His interests sit at the intersection of security operations, digital investigations, network and information security, fraud prevention, OSINT and emerging technologies.

Alongside his academic development, he has built and publicly documented early-stage technology concepts, turning ideas into working front-end prototypes, GitHub repositories, product documentation and investor-style narratives.

His approach focuses on translating technical security concepts into solutions that are understandable, usable and commercially relevant.
